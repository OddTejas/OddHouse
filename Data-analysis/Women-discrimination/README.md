Bihar is a state with high political representation but if you look at the data you would find is performing the worst in giving women freedom to work, in responsibilities regrading house work.  We are taught that poltical representation gives us a chance to express ourselves, so are women in Bihar not caring about there kind or are we taught the wrong thing?

While I was working on my  gender index, I stumbled upon a anomaly, Bihar has better political representation than the national average however it tops the chart for my report. 
## What I realized
- These women may not have the power to do anything. Yes, even though they  are elected into power they cannot take the decision. election does not equal empowerment high represntaion has not translated into lives of people. A newly elected MP cannot change the mindset of people overnight.
- Poor states like Bihar take a long time to change their economics landscape, the 2019 data used for socio-economic factor maybe reflecting the political landscape of 2000s while this report used 2023 political, though the political representation has not changed much for Bihar and remained similar. This means political support alone cannot change deep rooted habits.
### Takeaway
The political landscape is decoupled with the socio-economic life. This diffrence between high inequality and political repesentation tell a story of legislative power which does not translate into household empowerment.
## Karnataka story
If you look in Karnataka it has all round high inequality, Southern states are considered developed but Karnataka has comparable inequality to Assam.

- Karnataka does not have equal development in the entire state. Its capital Bengaluru is a software hub with high revenue generation for the state making it look wealthy but the rural parts of the city have little to no infrastructure to support employment to women and lacks safety for women. States can have a good average but while ignoring the vulnerable. This is the result of not having opportunities outside of few concentrated areas.
### Takeaway
Karnataka is being teated as one single unit where in reality the GDP of bengaluru and povery of rural villages   creates an average the data and called an Aggrigation bias
