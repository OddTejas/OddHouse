### 1. Temporal Misalignment in Data Sources
**Observation:** The dataset combines TUS (2019), PLFS (2019), and PRS (2023).
**Impact:** This creates temporal mismatch, where using two different timelines, however due to the lack of proper datasets due to their 2 year report period, matching data is not possible. The reports for political representation of women is 4 years ahead.              
**Notes for future:** For future iterations make sure the to wait for sycronized surveys to prevent this temporal lag. Until then, note the this mismatch and flag it.

---

### 2. Aim to measure the social fabric without gdp
**Observation:** The report uses criterias like workforce and representaion
**Impact:** While the idea is correct, Trying to measure social freedom without accounting for wealth is indirectly measuring poverty along with inequality. States who may have low workforce participation maybe due to them having poor infrastructure and transport for women discouraging them to work.
**Notes for future:**  Learn statistical tools to seperate wealth from inequality.
